# Receipt — painted-glyph contrast arbitration, full print scan with WCAG thresholds, and the per-owner label/name table (2026-10-15, continuation 3)

Everything here is at Product `d14da74c` (production-like local build, sandbox Chromium 153, served on 127.0.0.1:8099).

## 0. Method change that invalidates part of the earlier numbers

The first print scan compared an element's **computed** colour with the sampled background. That is wrong for two reasons, both now measured:

1. **alpha text** — a colour like `rgba(255,255,255,.35)` is not what reaches the eye; the painted result is a blend with the surface (`rgb(94,99,100)` in the home hero, i.e. 3.10:1, not the 1.9:1 the naive method reported);
2. **backgrounds that are images or alpha layers** — sampling a box that includes a picture or a faded overlay yields a luminance the text is not actually sitting on.

Method used from here: shoot the element **normally** (A) and with its text **transparent** (B); the pixel with the largest |A−B| difference is the glyph core (painted text colour), and the B pixels where A≈B give the true background.

## 3. axe reliability boundary (measured, not assumed)

axe's `color-contrast` matched the painted result for opaque text on a flat surface, but failed in three ways here:

- **1.18:1 for `.heart-flip-hint-text`** — alpha text over a painting; painted = **9.23:1**. False positive (withdrawn earlier).
- **3.42:1 for `#gbs2Pct`** — the pill was taken as background and the track as foreground; painted = **11.58:1**. False positive.
- **3.15:1 for `.article-byline__updated`** — directionally right, mildly optimistic; painted = **2.99:1**. True positive.

Rule for this project: axe stays the screen-media census, but any contrast *conclusion* on image-, gradient- or alpha-backed surfaces must use the painted-glyph method.

## Correction applied 2026-10-16 (self-correction SC-C1)

The `fn-marker` line in §4 is withdrawn. Measurement of `scripts/lib/note-registry.mjs` and of the printed endnotes shows the
