# Receipt — print-media contrast collapse, A4 overflow correction, and PDF text-layer limits (2026-10-15, continuation 2)

Sandbox Chromium 153, production-like build of Product `d14da74c`, served at 127.0.0.1:8099. Methods:

- **Pixel-truth contrast**: the element's text is made `color: transparent` (layout untouched), the element is screenshotted, and the pixels inside the text's own box are sampled; the ratio compares the sampled background luminance (median and 90th percentile) with the colour the text had before transparency. axe cannot do this — it inherits an ancestor colour when the real background is an image, which is exactly why the first pass could only call these pairs UNPROVEN.
- **Full-page print scan**: every element carrying >=12 characters of its own text is recorded with its box, the page is screenshotted in print media, and each box is sampled from the screenshot.

## 3. Method limit that must accompany every PDF-text claim

The cover captions above **are present** in the extracted PDF text layer (`биографиислужителей`, `хроникаверных`, `нагорнаяпроповедь…`, `тайнычеловеческогосердца`, `тёмнаясторонакафедры` all match after letters-only normalisation) although the rendered page shows them unreadable. Conversely, on `/articles/krajne-li-isporcheno-serdce/` page 2 the text layer omits the words «Нового Завета» that the rendered page displays.

Consequence: **Chromium's PDF text layer is not a reliable witness** — it can both omit displayed words and report invisible ones. Print claims in this pass rest on page renders (pdf.js + @napi-rs/canvas) and pixel sampling, never on text extraction alone.
