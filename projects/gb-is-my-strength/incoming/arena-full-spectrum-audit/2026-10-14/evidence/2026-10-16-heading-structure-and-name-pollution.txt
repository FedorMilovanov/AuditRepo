# Receipt — document outline and heading names: what assistive tech actually receives (2026-10-16, continuation 8)

Product `d14da74c`, production-like local build (108 HTML pages), Chromium 153 on 127.0.0.1:8099, axe-core 4.x injected per page, plus Chrome's own accessibility tree through CDP.

## 1. New defect: every section heading's accessible name carries a control's label

`src/runtime/article-heading-anchors.js:129-150` (`installArticleHeadingAnchors()`) selects every `h2[id], h3[id], h4[id]` (excluding `.summary-card`, `.author-card`) and **appends** the anchor *inside* the heading:

```js
anchor = document.createElement('a');
anchor.className = 'heading-anchor';
anchor.href = `#${heading.id}`;
heading.appendChild(anchor);            // <- inside the heading
anchor.setAttribute('aria-label', 'Скопировать ссылку на раздел');
```

Accessible-name-from-contents walks a heading's descendants, so a descendant's `aria-label` becomes part of the heading's name. Two independent witnesses agree:

| witness | result |
|---|---|
| Chrome's own AX tree (CDP `Accessibility.getFullAXTree`) on `/articles/kod-da-vinchi/` | 37 headings, **16 of them named** «‹текст раздела› Скопировать ссылку на раздел» |
| Playwright `ariaSnapshot()` on the same page | identical names, e.g. `heading "Введение Скопировать ссылку на раздел" [level=2]` |

Live DOM scan over 23 routes: **16 routes** place such controls inside headings — 224 controls in the sample, 2–38 per route (kod-da-vinchi 21, gill-1 27, gill-2 38, gill-spravochnik 16, krajne 29, sovetskaya-noch 21, hermenevtika 16, serdce-i-duh 15, nagornaya/chast-1 13, home 8, baptisty landing 8, about 5, nagornaya 2, genesis-6 2, rodosloviye 2, hard-texts 1). Each control is also its own tab stop inside a heading (`tabIndex=0`).

**The control itself works** — this is not a broken feature: clicking it copied `http://127.0.0.1:8099/articles/kod-da-vinchi/#sec-intro` to the clipboard and raised the toast «Ссылка на раздел скопирована». The defect is exactly its contribution to the heading name (and one extra tab stop per section).

## 2. New criteria: outline order and landmark containment (axe, moderate/best-practice)

`heading-order`: fires on **3 of 23** sampled routes, **8 nodes** — `/articles/kod-da-vinchi/` 6 (h2→h4 «Миф романа» / «Исторический факт»), `/articles/dzhon-gill-chast-1-chelovek/` 1, `/articles/dzhon-gill-chast-2-uchenyi/` 1 (h1→h4 card titles). Sitewide static: **5 of 108** pages contain level jumps (four Gill articles h1→h4; kod-da-vinchi six instances).

Separately, **64 of 108** pages open their outline with an `h2` that sits **outside** `<main>` and before the page `h1`: 63 × `h2.gbs2-current-title` in the series rail (`_shared/series/SeriesReaderChrome.astro`, `GillSeriesRail.astro:150`) and 1 × `h2.gbs2-rtitle` (`BaptistyRossiiBody.astro:9`). On `/baptisty-rossii/` the rail's h2 text is byte-identical to the page's h1 («Баптисты России»); the rail is visible (measured). Live, 7 of 23 sampled routes have at least one text-bearing heading before the h1.

`region`: fires on **14 of 23** routes, **22 nodes** — `#gb-ember-speed-*` (reader widget, injected at runtime), `#gbFloatingControls`, `#read-progress`, `.skip-link`, `.sdg` (the «Soli Deo Gloria» line), `.sr-only`, `section`, `.intro`, and five nodes on `/rodosloviye/` (`.genealogy-toolbar`, `.genealogy-navigation`, `.genealogy-status` — an interactive tool outside any landmark).
