  607 commits behind current main, so NONE is repair-ready on current HEAD without a fresh reverify.
- This is a deliberate evidence-freshness observation, NOT a claim that these bugs are fixed/open.
  Some of these rows are covered by SD-6 (map-engine fixes in PR #709 land on 2273b8c9):
