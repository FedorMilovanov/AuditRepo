## 1. Corrected finding set

### DP-017 — V84 mixed non-coordinate categories

- Status: `confirmed-current-on-research-branch`
- Evidence: V84 §0 placed `clinical syndrome`, causes/contexts, moral categories and crisis severity in one list.
- Root cause: classification level was treated as a peer of etiological and pastoral categories.
- Required correction: use separate axes; V84B supersedes the list.

### DP-018 — Clinical syndrome is not the primary ontology

- Status: `confirmed-principle`
- Finding: DSM/ICD can describe symptom configuration, duration, impairment, exclusions and safety. They do not define the human being before God.
- Required wording: Scripture/reformed anthropology first; clinical description subordinate.

### DP-019 — Depression belongs broadly to fallen-world misery

- Status: `confirmed-theological`
- Finding: depressive darkness is not part of original creation's `very good`; it exists in the world under sin, curse, corruption and death.
- Guardrail: broad fall-causality does not prove a specific personal sin in a specific sufferer.

### DP-020 — `Disease of the soul` is permitted only with definition

- Status: `qualified-green`
- Allowed meaning: a disorder/affliction of the inner person—mind, affections, conscience, hope and will—within an embodied person before God.
- Forbidden meaning: body-ignoring dualism, automatic guilt, or a claim that medical assessment is unnecessary.

### DP-021 — `Absence of God` requires covenantal distinction

- Status: `high-risk wording gate`
- True scope: the unregenerate person is objectively without God and alienated from Him.
- False scope: a depressed believer is automatically without God or no longer united to Christ.
- Required distinction: objective alienation / felt absence / fatherly hiding / bodily inability to feel consolation / unbelieving conclusion.

### DP-022 — A believer can be depressed without being abandoned

- Status: `confirmed-biblical-pastoral`
- Evidence locus: Isa. 50:10; Ps. 42/43; Ps. 88; Job; 2 Cor. 1:8–9; official MLJ Spiritual Depression corpus.
- Gate: lack of felt light must not be equated with lack of saving union.

### DP-023 — All depression is spiritually significant; not all is caused by personal sin

- Status: `qualified-green`
- Finding: depression always becomes a Godward arena because the sufferer is a person before God.
- Guardrail: spiritual significance is not identical to moral culpability or single-cause diagnosis.

### DP-024 — Five-axis model is mandatory

- Status: `implementation-required`
- Axes:
  1. theological ontology;
  2. experienced manifestations;
  3. clinical classification and safety;
  4. proximate causes/contributors;
  5. moral-pastoral response.
- Gate: no direct inference from diagnostic label to guilt, innocence, regeneration or apostasy.

### DP-025 — Lloyd-Jones full-text status changed, but only partially

- Status: `evidence-status-correction`
- Book: `BOOK-FULLTEXT-HOLD` remains.
- Official sermon corpus: `OFFICIAL-SERMON-SUMMARY-READ / AUDIO-AVAILABLE`.
- Verified themes: real depressed Christians; final spiritual role of unbelief; physical condition and temperament; danger of confusing physical/psychological/spiritual; multiple distinct causes; gospel/doctrinal remedies.
- Forbidden: claiming the book was page-read or all 24 audios were completed.

## 3. Mandatory implementation wording

### Site opening hierarchy

1. Depression is first discussed as darkness/affliction of the fallen whole person before God.
2. The body, brain, sleep, illness and medicine may shape the experience.
3. Clinical classification may describe a threshold pattern and safety needs.
4. The counselor still asks about suffering, guilt, faith, false guilt, temptation and response.

### Required covenantal distinction

- Unregenerate: objectively without God in the world.
- Believer: may experience darkness and felt absence, but is not thereby outside Christ.

### Required sentence-level guardrails

- `The fall explains why such misery exists; it does not identify the specific personal cause in every case.`
- `A diagnosis is neither a moral verdict nor a certificate of innocence.`
- `Disease of the soul does not mean disease outside the body.`
- `Felt absence of God is not the same as actual abandonment by God.`
