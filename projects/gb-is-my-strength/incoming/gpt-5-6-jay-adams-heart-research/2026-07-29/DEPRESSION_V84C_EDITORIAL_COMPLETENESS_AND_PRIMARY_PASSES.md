## 2. New governed findings

### DP-027 — David omission is a material balance defect

The current site draft strongly protects sufferers from automatic moral accusation but lacks a separate reader-facing case where Scripture explicitly relates darkness to real hidden guilt.

Required insertion:

- Psalm 31/32 — silence, wasting bones, groaning, heavy hand;
- Psalm 37/38 — sin as burden, bodily and inward distress;
- Psalm 50/51 — confession, cleansing, renewed heart, restored joy.

No retrospective modern diagnosis is permitted.

### DP-028 — diagnosis and comprehensive assessment must not be collapsed

A diagnostic label/category does not by itself establish:

- severity;
- course;
- functional impairment;
- risk;
- bipolar history;
- physical/neurological contributors;
- substances/medications;
- relational and social context.

NICE requires a comprehensive assessment not based merely on symptom count.

### DP-029 — Goodwin supplies a missing epistemic guardrail

In *A Child of Light Walking in Darkness*, Isaiah 50 darkness is not known sinful practice or ignorance. A real child of God may lack felt testimony of divine favour and may misread promises, providence and correction through fear, conscience and accusation.

Governed inference:

> The intensity and subjective certainty of self-condemnation do not authenticate it as God's verdict.

### DP-030 — disease of the soul remains qualified whole-person language

Allowed only when it means the suffering inner person in embodied unity.

Forbidden inferences:

- body irrelevant;
- every symptom volitional;
- soul-language proves personal guilt;
- spiritual care excludes medical assessment.

### DP-031 — grief, burnout and depressive disorder are not synonyms

- grief is a response to loss and may be faithful lament;
- prolonged grief disorder is a separate classification;
- ICD-11 burnout is an occupational phenomenon, not a universal medical condition;
- depressive disorder is a clinical category requiring assessment.

Overlap does not erase distinction.

### DP-032 — crisis wording was too narrow

Site language must include at least:

- suicidal intent or plan;
- recent attempt;
- inability to maintain immediate safety;
- psychosis;
- mania with major loss of judgment;
- threat to others;
- severe self-neglect / inability to provide basic care.

This remains a referral boundary, not a public self-triage algorithm.

### DP-033 — Lloyd-Jones whole-person findings strengthened

Official MLJ Trust pages support:

- real spiritual depression in genuine Christians;
- conversion does not erase temperament;
- physical exhaustion or illness may be misread as spiritual decline;
- physical, psychological and spiritual realms interact and can be confused;
- gospel application addresses mind, heart and will;
- past forgiven sin may continue as self-condemnation through deficient grasp of justification and union with Christ;
- weariness may combine bodily overwork, carnal energy, wrong motive and ministry identity.

Book edition remains `BOOK-FULLTEXT-HOLD`; official sermon pages are a separate evidence class.

### DP-034 — confession must proceed to cleansing and Gospel assurance

A David section may not end with exposure of guilt. It must continue through Psalm 51 to cleansing, renewed heart and restored joy.

Forbidden drift:

- endless introspection after specific confession;
- treating emotional delay as proof that forgiveness failed;
- demanding immediate felt joy as evidence of genuine repentance.
