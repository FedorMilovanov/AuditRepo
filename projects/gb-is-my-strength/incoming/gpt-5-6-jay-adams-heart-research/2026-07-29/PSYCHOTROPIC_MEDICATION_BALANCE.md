## 5. Publication gates

### GATE A — theological primacy

Pass only if:

- Scripture, not psychiatry, defines heart, sin, repentance and sanctification;
- medicine is not described as soul-cure;
- symptom relief is not mocked or denied;
- gospel and church care remain active whether medication is used or not.

### GATE B — body reality

Pass only if:

- organic illness and bodily weakness remain possible and important;
- adequate medical evaluation is encouraged when indicated;
- no suffering person is accused of hidden sin merely because symptoms persist;
- bodily care is not framed as spiritual failure.

### GATE C — medication safety

Pass only if:

- no starting/stopping/dose instruction is addressed to the reader;
- abrupt withdrawal is explicitly rejected;
- benefits, harms, side effects and withdrawal are individualized;
- dependence is not equated automatically with addiction;
- class-specific claims carry a current source.

### GATE D — competence

Pass only if:

- medical decisions remain with patient + qualified prescriber;
- counselor’s role is spiritual care, observation, questions, referral and support;
- neither pastor nor article poses as a physician;
- physician does not receive authority over doctrine of man or soul.

### GATE E — crisis and safeguarding

Pass only if:

- suicidal intent/plan, psychosis, mania, violence risk, severe intoxication/withdrawal and inability to care for self are routed to urgent professional help;
- abuse is not treated as a mere communication or mood problem;
- medication discussion never delays immediate safety action.

### GATE F — source integrity

Pass only if:

- exact quotes come from full text and exact locator;
- TMSJ/Hodges PDF quotes remain HOLD until page-image verification;
- official book pages are not used as proof of unseen chapters;
- old pharmacological claims are not treated as current without M1 verification.

## 6. Forbidden publication drift

Do not publish any of the following:

- `all psychiatric diagnosis is fiction`;
- `all depression is personal sin`;
- `a faithful Christian never needs medication`;
- `medication proves weak faith`;
- `medication cures the heart`;
- `a biological label removes moral/spiritual agency`;
- `all psychotropics are addictive in the same way`;
- `antidepressant withdrawal and benzodiazepine addiction are the same category`;
- `stop taking the drug and trust God`;
- `because a doctor prescribed it, no spiritual discernment is needed`;
- `because a counselor sees a heart issue, no physician is needed`;
- any individual diagnosis or treatment recommendation from the public article.
