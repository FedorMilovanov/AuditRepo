## 1. Audit trigger

The earlier Site wording correctly denied an ontological rupture within the Trinity, but compressed a highly complex question into a single sentence. That compression carried opposite risks:

- weakening the reality of judgment, curse, dereliction and substitution;
- or describing the cross as a quarrel, loss of love, division of divine essence or collapse of Trinitarian unity.

The correction therefore had to preserve all load-bearing doctrines at once rather than solve one risk by creating another.

---

## 2. Governed findings

### TC-001 — The saving work is Trinitarian

Supported biblical sequence:

- the Father sends and does not spare the Son;
- the Son voluntarily gives Himself;
- the incarnate Son offers Himself to God through the eternal Spirit.

Disposition:

`INTEGRATE`

Boundary:

The cross is not the Father's will defeating a resistant Son. Personal distinctions remain real, while the divine saving will and action are not divided into rival projects.

### TC-002 — Judgment and curse are real

The cry of dereliction must not be reduced to theatre, a literary citation without experiential content, or a generic name for psychological loneliness.

Disposition:

`INTEGRATE`

Boundary:

Christ truly bears judgment, curse, sin and death as Substitute. The article does not define the exact metaphysical mechanism beyond Scripture.

### TC-003 — The subject of suffering is the Son

The sufferer is not a separate human person alongside the eternal Son.

Required formula:

> The same eternal Son truly suffered and died for us according to the human nature He assumed.

Boundary:

- no Nestorian separation of subjects;
- no claim that the divine nature became mortal, corruptible or passible;
- no weakening of the unity of Christ's Person.

### TC-004 — `No rupture` is a guardrail, not a reduction

The denial of an ontological rupture means:

- the divine essence did not divide;
- Father, Son and Spirit did not cease to be the one God;
- the Father did not cease to love the Son;
- the eternal personal relations did not collapse;
- God's unified will and saving action did not become contradictory.

It does not mean:

- the cry expressed a mere mistake;
- judgment and curse were unreal;
- the Son did not enter the deepest redemptive sorrow;
- the cross was only an appearance.

### TC-005 — `The Father turned away` requires qualification

This shorthand is permitted only when it means that the Father did not deliver the incarnate Son from the cup, the Son was deprived of sensible consolation and the penal sentence was truly borne.

It is prohibited when it implies cessation of love, division of divine essence, competing divine wills or destruction of Trinitarian unity.

### TC-006 — Revelation sets the stopping point

The project may confess:

- one God in three Persons;
- personal distinction without separation;
- voluntary obedience of the Son;
- real penal and substitutionary suffering;
- one Person in two natures;
- suffering and death according to the assumed human nature;
- immutability of the divine nature.

The project may not publish a confident map of the inaccessible `inner mechanism` of the Trinity at the moment of the cry.

Disposition:

`MYSTERY BOUNDARY / NO METAPHYSICAL OVERCLAIM`
