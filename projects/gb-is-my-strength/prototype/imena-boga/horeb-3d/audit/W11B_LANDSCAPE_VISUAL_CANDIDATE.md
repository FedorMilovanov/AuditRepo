# W11B — Midian visual environment overhaul (staged candidate)

This staged tree is deliberately not published while W11A.2 QA is running.
It removes the artificial rows of spherical green 'trees' and balloon
boulders seen in independent Chromium screenshots, replaces them with
sparse instanced woody scrub and irregular erosion-scatter, and introduces
two independent annular sandstone ridge meshes to give real 360-degree
landscape depth, not a flat screenshot background.

The physical ground, well and trough remain unchanged. The well's existing
96 discrete stones receive a narrower natural stone palette and tiny
per-block height/angle irregularity rather than a regular checkerboard.
Staging preserves active PR head 66e04fd and all Windows W10D edits.

from first-person and wide/mobile framing, six HOREB QA jobs, Validate,
Preflight, file://, W2 real input, sacred radii, all 42 dossiers.
