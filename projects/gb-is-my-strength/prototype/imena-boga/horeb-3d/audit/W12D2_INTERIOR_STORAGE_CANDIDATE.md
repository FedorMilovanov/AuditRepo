## Independent, low-cost artistic candidate
- Two small **anonymous hypothetical** dry-storage baskets sit against opposite back-side areas of the existing family shelter. They are **not** asserted to be Moses', Zipporah's, or Jethro's actual possessions, and depict no action or character.
- Each basket is one shared bounded `LatheGeometry` with real woven vertex tones, one horizontal rim and one dark interior disk. Names and actual counts are exposed through existing canvas dataset. No external assets, physical simulations, audio, archaeological textures or additional narrative.
- The two basket locations/radii are exported and reused by the real walk collider. A person cannot pass through the same objects they see; all centre-entry coordinates stay walkable. These are large enough to merit simple matching colliders, unlike the 720 W12D.1 grit-scale pebbles.
- Add 2 unit gates for safe placement/collisions, and one browser scene contract for 2 actual bodies and 2 visible rim meshes. Keep real mobile touch, rAF readiness, 42 dossiers, physical entrance, WebGL dispose and four screenshot gates.
- No change to Nile, Midian well, Horeb scene, Research, original user W10D or rights.

## QA preflight refinement
The basket lathe has 28 angular segments. A theta*27 weave tone aliases to one broad color band on the polygon vertices, not a fine weave. Changed vertex frequency to theta*9, within the angular sample resolution; preserved theta*13 cross-weft component. No geometry or collision change.
