# W12D.5.1 — replace failed dark basket hoops with profile-following fibres

## Direct actual-Chromium visual rejection
On exact W12D.5 `820a1c9`, an independent Chrome SwiftShader `file://` preview was executed on Windows in a clean, isolated Downloads folder (no checkout, no GPU use, no W10D edits). Desktop, walkable interior and mobile390 screenshots were captured without JS errors. Witness: two baskets, two rims, ten earlier torus bindings in one instanced mesh, two roof shadow casters, zero self-shadow receivers and one ground bounce light. The `walk-interior.png` inspection shows **five dark nearly horizontal hoops per basket**, resembling mechanical barrel fittings, far more visible than the woven body. Therefore W12D.5 is an ART FAIL despite source/geometry success.

## Narrow and systematic repair
Replace those rejected hoop rings with 8 fine, slightly rising/leaning profile-bound fibre lines per existing basket, using a single shared TubeGeometry and a single InstancedMesh (16 instances, 1 draw call). Approximate body radius is derived from the **same** authored LatheGeometry profile at sampled heights; visual skin offset and tube thickness are strictly bounded. No new shadow map, maps, object or historical assertion. Existing basket geometry, materials and coordinates are unchanged; same exact collider radii and open entrance.
