# W12D.6 — softer anisotropic woven texture without eight-pixel grid

## Observed from exact W12D.5.1 CI screenshots
After W12D.5.1's targeted basket fix passed 6/6 and was visually accepted, close inspection of the **rear wall** of real `homestead-walk-interior.png` (artifact `horeb-chromium-witness-44341542105`, #14445203751) still shows an overly even crossed yarn pattern. This issue is **separate** from the already accepted W12D.4 roof light and W12D.5.1 basket detail.

## Restrained candidate change
The single existing `128x128` procedurally authored shared texture keeps `repeatU=6`, `repeatV=4`, `bumpScale=.007` and its existing Nine material bindings. W12D.6 changes **only the per-texel brightness calculation**:
- replace the fixed, exactly aligned `cos(xπ/4)` and `cos(yπ/4)` yarn peaks with small smooth 2D phase drifts generated by integer Fourier modes of x/y;
- reduce rigid crossing amplitudes, add subdued periodic low-frequency variation while **preserving exact tile seams**;
- keep the same 1 texture, no network assets, extra meshes, draw calls, new lights, GPU maps, UV changes, collisions or historical claim about textiles.

A new automated source contract samples the 8-texel pattern across the whole tile and requires a non-zero per-stripe phase difference, while retaining earlier W12D.3 & W12D.3.1 contrast/mipmap/tile tests. It must NOT be judged solely by numerical tests.
