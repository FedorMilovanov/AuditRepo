- Smoothly move the upper part behind the real roof edge and keep all vertices within the exact original back-wall collision band. Vertex color and normal gradients are computed on one mesh; no new texture, light, physics object, component, audio, network asset or historically unproven decoration.
- Triangle count on rear grows 880 → 1080, while **draw calls and bound fabric materials reduce by one**. Existing fixed shared 128² tile, roof/side wall, posts, baskets, physical entrance and four independent walk routes remain unchanged.
