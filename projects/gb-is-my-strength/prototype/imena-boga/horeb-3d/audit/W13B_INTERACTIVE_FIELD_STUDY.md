# W13B — spatial field observation, learning mastery and interactable object pilot

Two real physical locations (Midian well and Midian family shelter) each expose one source-cited field study only when the grounded visitor approaches the object. Direct evidence from Exodus 2:16–21 is kept separate from illustrative well masonry, shelter shape or textile: no historical claims about a named excavation, species, palace, date, costume or figure of God.

The system is purposeful gameplay, not a decorative modal: the engine itself pauses all movement and pointer dragging at its CURRENT camera location; readers can compare alternatives and revisit the text. The two contexts are independent, and a sanitized local-only journal stores observed/understood plus capped wrong attempts. Answering wrong yields a meaningful evidence hint; repeating an error names the precise reference; the visitor can then retry. Focus enters the dialog, Tab cycles within it, Escape/Close resumes the world and returns focus. Reduced-motion WebGL remount re-applies the pause. The legacy 42 dossiers, existing 7 theologico-textual lessons and all other scenes remain unchanged.
