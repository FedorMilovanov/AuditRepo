# Source reverify — Exodus 3:14–15 / YHWH etymology

**Status: exact correction of two localized errors; NOT whole-corpus editorial approval.**

## Incorrect verse attribution in Arena B

Earlier Reader source attributed a composite quote starting «Сущий послал меня к вам…» to **Исход 3:15**. In Russian Synodal, that phrase belongs to **3:14**, while 3:15 introduces the LORD / the God of the patriarchs and ends with the declaration about the divine name. A quote assembled across verses must not be labelled a verbatim quotation from one verse.

Evidence:
- https://www.bible-center.ru/ru/bibletext/synnew_ru/ex/3%3A14-15
- https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=%D0%98%D1%81%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B4+3%3A15&version=RUSV

**Candidate correction:** B YHWH verse now quotes only a short correct excerpt from Exodus 3:15; B Ehyeh remains independently anchored to 3:14. A YHWH source excerpt similarly separates its Hebrew word note from the short Russian translation instead of embedding a Hebrew bracket inside the quote.

## Theological exegesis ≠ certain historical etymology

Exodus 3 draws a verbal relationship to `אֶהְיֶה` (first-person of Hebrew `היה`), but the exact original morphology/etymology of the Tetragrammaton is debated, and not all proposed meanings reduce to «He is».

Independent interpretative sources:
- SBL John J. Collins *Introduction to the Hebrew Bible*: https://www.sbl-site.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/IntrotoHebrewBible_FP.pdf
- USCCB Exodus 3 notes: https://bible.usccb.org/bible/exodus/3
- American Bible Society *Lord (YHWH)*: https://www.americanbible.org/engage/bible-resources/articles/lord-yhwh/

Candidate A/B correct categorical derivations by marking historical uncertainty. The LXX Exodus 3:14 has `ἐγώ εἰμι ὁ ὤν`, whereas John 8:58 has `ἐγώ εἰμί`. Connection is theologically relevant but the full clauses are not verbatim identical.
