# Professional editorial and agent-workflow research — broad wave 23

**Date:** 2026-10-29  
**Method:** 13 professional/open documentation repositories were cloned at immutable commits; 64 individual guides/templates were read and compared with Product policy and the live publication case. URLs below use immutable GitHub blobs. This is a technique review, not a popularity list.

## Synthesis for `gb-is-my-strength`

The corpus converges on six techniques that are applicable here:

1. **Typed content model:** distinguish article/series/hub/special-app forms and their required anatomy.
2. **One owner per reusable fact/projection:** derive discovery surfaces rather than duplicate them manually.
3. **Purpose-specific checks:** deterministic lint/schema/link/discovery checks plus human source, language and theology review.
4. **Pre-publication transaction:** plan → research → draft → editorial review → atomic projection → exact-head verification.
5. **Short operational context, deep reference on demand:** root/router + path-specific contract, not 54 standards in working memory.
6. **Measured agent improvement:** negative fixtures and historical/live cohorts, not claims that a longer prompt is safer.

## Rejected “improvements”

- Merge all policies into one monolith — contradicted by typed-content and progressive-disclosure practice.
- Add another universal checklist — duplicates existing rules without closing transaction ownership.
- Auto-rewrite Russian/theological prose from lint — deterministic tooling cannot own meaning or source fidelity.
- Force every article into one visual template — content models standardize contracts, not all presentation.
- Replace editorial review with green CI — professional sources consistently separate automatable correctness from judgment.

## Evidence-backed recommendation

Keep the current editorial depth. Make it usable through one article/series authoring entrypoint that selects the relevant model, generates/validates the projection plan, and leaves source/theological/language decisions to explicit human review. Add only the authority-order contradiction lane and extend the already admitted atomic publication lane; the research does not justify further Product backlog rows by itself.
