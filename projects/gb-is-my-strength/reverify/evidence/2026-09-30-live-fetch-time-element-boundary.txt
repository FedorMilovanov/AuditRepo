## Why this receipt exists

The 2026-09-30 pass recorded a "visible on live d0e04a9c" column for the Baptist byline dates.
That column cannot be produced by this tool: fetch_page drops the text content of <time> elements
and instead surfaces the hidden Pagefind meta spans. Any live claim about a human-readable byline
label therefore needs a rendering browser or a raw-HTML retrieval, or an exact-SHA built artifact.

## Conclusion

- The two ISO strings per page are the hidden spans inserted by scripts/lib/editorial-metadata-v3.js
  (projectPagefindDates): data-pagefind-meta="publishedTime" and "modifiedTime".
  Proof: the values extracted from live equal, byte for byte, the hidden spans of the local
  production-like build of the live SHA d0e04a9c (see 2026-09-30-baptist-dates-main-vs-live-sha.txt).
- fetch_page therefore cannot witness visible byline labels; it CAN witness ordinary element text
  (the "????" copy loss above is a valid live witness) and it CAN witness the projected machine dates.
- Positive fact obtained here: the live release already carries the 2026-09-08 owner-approved
  exact-instant editorial metadata (e.g. spravochnik modifiedTime 2026-08-20T11:04:03.000Z), i.e.
  live and main agree on the editorial projection.
- Not obtained here: any live confirmation of what the human-readable byline says. That is now
  witnessed from the exact-SHA built artifact instead (both SHAs, builds exit 0).
