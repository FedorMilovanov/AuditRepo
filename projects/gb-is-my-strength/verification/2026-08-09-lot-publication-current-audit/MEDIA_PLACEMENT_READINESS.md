## Why the declared gate and current plan disagree

#1339 says final browser evidence must exercise **all 14 raster illustrations**.

The current placement plan explicitly keeps five of the 14 conceptual families out of publication placement and currently renders only six of the remaining nine.

There are two legitimate end states, but one must be chosen explicitly:

### A. Preserve the 14-visible-image publication contract

- complete metadata for all 14;
- place all 14 in semantically appropriate sections;
- create all 42 responsive WebP variants + dedicated OG;
- browser-test all 14 rendered figures.

### B. Reduce the visible-image count deliberately

If editorial judgment says 14 images would over-illustrate the article, then:

- owner explicitly changes the publication/media acceptance boundary from 14 to the accepted smaller count;
- only assets that will actually be published are generated and retained;
- browser witness asserts that exact accepted positive count;
- unused “reserve” concepts are not described as delivered publication families;
- the PR/body/checklist no longer claims “all 14 raster illustrations”.

What is **not** acceptable is a third state where 14 assets are claimed/generated while only 6–9 are wired into the article and the browser marathon silently iterates the smaller DOM set.

## Correct closure checks

For the final media/placement transaction require:

1. declared expected visible figure count equals actual DOM `[data-lot-figure]` count;
2. declared expected asset-family count equals registry count;
3. every registered published family has exactly the required 600/900/1200 bytes;

9. branch is refreshed to then-current main and exact-head checks are rerun.
