# Numayra excavation-date audit correction — 2026-08-09

## Supersession

This record **supersedes the earlier `FALSE-POSITIVE / DO NOT PROMOTE` disposition** for the Lot Sources wording about Numayra excavation dates in `REPORT.md`.

Final current disposition:

`LOT-NUMAYRA-DATE-01 = PARTIAL/NARROWED / P3 SOURCE-ANNOTATION PRECISION`.

The earlier pass stopped at the linked EDSP Numayra page. That page does indeed say that approximately 30% of the extant site was excavated “from 1979–1983,” so the Lot wording was not fabricated. A broader primary/academic trace, however, proves that actual Numayra excavation work also occurred in **1977**.

## Current Lot wording

`LotSectionSources.astro` source 4 says:

> `Numayra. Сводка EDSP по укреплённому поселению, раскопкам 1979–1983 годов и радиоуглеродной хронологии`

and links the current EDSP Numayra page.

That annotation is narrowly faithful to one sentence on the linked page, but read as a summary of the excavation campaign it omits the 1977 field season.

## Evidence

### Linked EDSP page — supports the narrow wording

The current official/project page `NUMAYRA – Expedition Dead Sea Plain` states:

- Numayra is a 0.5 hectare walled settlement about 13 km south of Bab edh-Dhra’;
- calibrated radiocarbon dates place its occupation/abandonment in EB III;
- approximately 30% of the extant site (c.1500 m²) was excavated **from 1979–1983**.

So `1979–1983` is a real phrase in the cited project summary.

### Broader excavation record — proves 1977 fieldwork

Independent primary/academic evidence shows the excavation campaign was not limited to that interval:

- the American Center of Research archive has items explicitly titled **“Jordan. Numeira excavations”**, dated **1977**, from the Expedition to the Dead Sea Plain dig photography;
- the scholarly article *Population aggregation, residential storage and socioeconomic inequality at Early Bronze Age Numayra, Jordan* thanks excavation staff from the **1977, 1979, 1981 and 1983 seasons**;
- the archaeological publication record includes Rast's `Settlement at Numeira` in the interim report of the **1977 season**.

The clean interpretation is therefore:

- `1979–1983` can describe the major/extant-site excavation span singled out by the current EDSP webpage;
- `1977, 1979, 1981, 1983` is the broader field-season history of excavation at Numayra.

## Why this is not a major factual defect

The body of the Lot article does **not** build any theological, chronological or identification argument on the start year of excavation. The issue lives only in the bibliography annotation.

Nothing in this correction changes the article's proper archaeological boundary that Numayra is an Early Bronze settlement and that archaeological destruction/location alone does not prove a biblical city name.

## Correct repair

Prefer a wording that preserves both truths, for example semantically:

- “сводка EDSP по Нумейре, включая основные раскопки 1979–1983 годов; проект также имеет материалы сезона 1977 года”;

or simply avoid summarizing the excavation years in the bibliography label unless the complete season list is given.

Do not alter the archaeological argument, radiocarbon discussion, or identification boundary merely to repair this source annotation.

## Audit lesson

When an official project landing page compresses its own field history, verification must distinguish:

1. whether the article accurately paraphrases that page; and
2. whether the paraphrase is complete enough to stand as a general historical statement.

The first Lot audit proved (1) and incorrectly treated that as sufficient to close (2).
