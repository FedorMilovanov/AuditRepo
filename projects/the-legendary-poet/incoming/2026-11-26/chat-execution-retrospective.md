## Class-level repair: outcome-bound execution (not a new bureaucracy)

For direct-action requests, identify target + destination + complete requested outcome (e.g. title, description, tags, timing, comments); check the applicable canonical executor and required gates early. Routine steps within explicit scope do not require the owner to repeat approval or supply internal digests. Do not ignore real tool-required confirmation, rights, independent review, safety refusal or provider gates. Do not use a paid intermediary when the owner requires the canonical API. A new independent task is not a way to bypass a rejected action.

Preserve already verified effects, avoid duplicate writes and distinguish `verified`, `prepared`, `not_dispatched`, `may_exist`, and `blocked` per requested deliverable. At the end, say what was actually done, what remains and the specific boundary; provide a concise exact-ID/path receipt. An issue/PR is not the requested provider effect. A tool return, local copy, archive, upload, schedule, public release, first comment, additional comment and pin are different states. Never promise another agent will act without observed acceptance.

For physical artifact handoff, prefer the explicitly requested destination. Verify original identity and actual destination existence/size/hash. Do not mix curated candidates, owner-selected masters, rejected media, archival sources and synthetic reconstructions; maintain roles and exact references in the existing project manifest/storage layer. Never claim a sandbox file exists on the owner computer. If no authorized transfer exists, deliver a verified archive as an explicitly partial fallback; no new regeneration, public push of private assets, or background promise.

For editorial work, read the owning standards; verify semantic paragraphs and public presentation as well as raw text, avoid unsupported source additions, distinguish ASR from actual listening and source photograph from generated imagery. A current source/rights/review HOLD is not a software bug to suppress.
